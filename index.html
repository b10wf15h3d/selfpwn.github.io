// about

About the guide

Self Pwning started life as a talk by Sam Macdonald on mental health in cyber security. This field guide turns that talk into something you can keep: the ideas, the warning signs, and the worksheets, in a format you can read on your phone or print and pass on.

It's free to share. Print it for your team, leave it in the break room, or send it to someone who needs it more than you do.

This guide is written by security people, not mental health professionals. It's not a substitute for professional help. If you're struggling, please talk to a GP, doctor or therapist.
